# SheetHead
The head of a sheet: the headline in `h-sheet` on the left, the paragraph on the right, bottoms aligned.

**Use** to open every content sheet after the banner. The headline is a statement; a second clause may sit at `dim`. The paragraph is the sheet's one explanatory sentence or two, in `lede`.

**Provide** the headline (and optional dim clause) and the paragraph. Pair with SheetTab.

**Don't** centre, add an eyebrow above the headline (the tab is the eyebrow), or let the paragraph exceed 46ch.

**Kicker**: the lede may set above the headline in the left column instead (`kicker`, lede size, a gap of 20 to 32px before the headline) when the right column carries the answer to it: on the puzzle sheet the problem stands over "Beyond the obvious." and the paragraph on the right sets at lede size beside the glyph, the words it wants seen as **terms** (`term`: an inverted block, ink on ground in either theme, flipped on contrast and signal grounds). Home page only.

**Aside**: when the headline is tall (a stair, or two lines at full size), the right column may stack content under the lede (`head-aside`: the lede at `measure-lede`, then a glyph-and-paragraph row or similar), so the column beside the headline is filled rather than holding the lede alone at its foot. Home page only; every other sheet head is headline and lede.
