# SheetHead
The head of a sheet: the headline in `h-sheet` on the left, the paragraph on the right, bottoms aligned.

**Use** to open every content sheet after the banner. The headline is a statement; a second clause may sit at `dim`. The paragraph is the sheet's one explanatory sentence or two, in `lede`.

**Provide** the headline (and optional dim clause) and the paragraph. Pair with SheetTab.

**Don't** centre, add an eyebrow above the headline (the tab is the eyebrow), or let the paragraph exceed 46ch.
