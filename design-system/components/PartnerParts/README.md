# PartnerParts
The partner-portal parts from section 10 (reference CSS: `src/app/(partner)/partner.css`, loaded over section 09's `portal.css`, whose chrome, forms, flashes and ledgers the partner portal shares). Two audiences again: partner users get the customer's sheet rhythm, fewer and larger things; K7 staff get the ledger. Built from M1 (2026-10-22, P1–P6), M2 (2026-10-22, P7–P10), M3 (2026-10-22, P11–P12), M4 (2026-10-22, P13–P16), M5 (2026-10-23, P17–P19), M6 (2026-10-23, P20–P21) and M7 (2026-10-23, P22–P24); parts are added as the milestones in `docs/partner-portal/02-build-plan.md` land.

**P1 Partner ID (`pid`)** the identifier reads like the case number: `K7P-` dim, the number first and full weight, mono (`Pid`, on `tid`); `tid-display` is the large form on the partner's home and on an organisation page. Deal IDs (`K7D-`) and certificate IDs (`K7P-C-`, `K7P-O-`) take the same shape when they arrive.

**P2 Organisation state (`OrgStateTag`, `TypeTag`)** a `Tag` for the organisation: Approved solid, Suspended on `tag-signal`, because that is the one state that stops the partner. The partner type is a quiet outlined tag; no hue per type.

**P3 Status strip (`pstrip`)** the organisation's facts on hairlines, read before anything else on a home page: a `dl` of label-over-value cells in a row (type, status, your roles, certificate; on the K7 side: signed in as, roles, unread, staff listed), divided by hairlines, stacked on a phone. Values at 17px; a tag sits as a value.

**P4 The bell (`bell`, `bell-n`) and its rows (`ntf`)** the bell is the word *Notifications* in the header with the unread count as a block on `signal`, zero-padded (`03`), only while something is unread; on a phone the word hides and the block stays. The rows: relative time in mono on the left, the subject in semibold with the pixel before it while unread, the body in `muted` beneath; read rows drop to medium weight. The same list carries the organisation's recent record on the home page (date, action, from → to, who).

**P5 Role tags (`roles`, `tag-dashed`)** a person's roles as outlined tags in a row: Partner Admin · Sales · Technical for partner users, the five K7 roles for staff. A role is not a state, so nothing inverts; "No role" is dashed.

**P6 Partner home (`phome`)** the sheet after the strip: the latest notifications and the record in the main column, the organisation's facts (`meta-rows`) in the 360px side column from 1080px. Deals, collateral and schemes sit in the column.

**P7 The join steps (`jsteps`)** the public application's four steps on the customer track's bars (section 09 `track`): type · company and contact · verify · terms and submit, each cell its index in `acc` mono and its name; the current step's bar is signal, done steps are ink and link back, steps to come are line. A finished application shows every bar in ink. No percentage, no circles.

**P8 Application state (`AppStateTag`)** one `Tag` per state of 01-requirements §3.2: Submitted on signal for the K7 queue (someone must act) and Clarification needed on signal for the applicant (the same rule from their side), Under review solid state, Approved solid, Rejected closed, Draft dashed. `MobileVerifiedTag` says whether the mobile was verified by SMS or skipped for want of a gateway, as a quiet tag the Channel Manager reads. The application number `K7A-00001` (`Aid`) takes P1's shape.

**P9 The application summary (`app-summary`)** the application read back as filed facts: two groups of `meta-rows` (company; primary contact), side by side from 900px, the same component on the terms step (with Edit links on each group), on the applicant's status page and on the K7 sheet (which adds the terms version and the submission date). Verification sits beside the email and the mobile as tags.

**P10 The decision panel (`decide`)** in the side column of the K7 application sheet: the decisions the state allows as a stacked `choice` (approve · send back · reject, with one line each on what follows), the note to the applicant, one primary action. Hairline-filed like the terms box (`terms-box`) on the join page, which holds the acceptance check with the terms version in it. The desk strip (`K7Nav`, section 09's `wsbar`) carries a count block (`rtab-n`) on a tab with new work; the settings page's staff list is a `dtable` of inputs (`staff-table`).

**P11 User state (`UserStateTag`)** one `Tag` per state of a partner user: Active solid, Invited dashed (the row exists, the person has not arrived), Disabled closed. Never signal: no one must act on a user's state. The users ledger (`members`, a `dtable`) sets the person's name over their email and designation, their roles as P5 tags, the state, the last sign-in; a disabled row drops to `muted`. The member sheet is the form (name, designation, mobile, roles as checks; the email is the sign-in identity and is shown disabled) with the levers in the side column on the decision panel (P10): resend the invitation, disable or re-enable, and removal behind a confirming check.

**P12 The legal-identity edit (`pending`, `ledit`, `LegalChanges`)** a change to the legal name, GSTIN or PAN is not made but proposed (FR-REG-07): it sits in a hairline-filed box (`pending`) headed by the pixel, with who proposed it, when and why, and the change as a diff (`ledit`): one row per field, the label in mono `muted`, the current value struck through in `muted`, the arrow in `acc`, the proposed value at medium weight. The same box on the partner's organisation page (with *Withdraw*) and on the K7 partner sheet, where it carries the decision form (approve · decline, the note). The organisation page's two edit forms sit under the filed facts (`org-forms`): the details save at once, the legal form is disabled while an edit waits.

**P13 Deal state (`DealStateTag`, `Did`)** one `Tag` per state of 01-requirements §7.2: on signal where someone must act now (the K7 queue: Submitted and Extension requested; the partner: Information requested), Approved solid, Rejected, Expired and Withdrawn closed, the rest solid state. The deal number `K7D-00001` (`Did`) takes P1's shape, on the queue rows, the sheet head and inside the thread.

**P14 The registration form (`deal-form`, `lines-table`)** one post, no script to speak of: the end customer's facts, then *What is sold* as a small `dtable` of product lines (a select of the catalogue's active products grouped by family and a quantity each, six rows, blank rows dropped), the value, the closure date, the source as a select, the competitor, the notes and the files. It is the partner side's one client component so a refusal comes back with every field as typed and the errors under them (`field-error`), never an empty form. After a request for information the same form carries the filed values, K7's note above it in the pending box (P12) and a reply field in place of the notes. The deals list filters (`deal-filters`, `filters-grid`) sit in a `details` under the view switch: product, registered from and to, value from and to.

**P15 The conflicts box (`conflicts`, `ConflictBox`)** the pending box (P12) on the K7 deal sheet, first thing in the main column: the pixel in signal and the count in the head while there is something to read, each conflict a bell row (P4) with the other deal's number, its customer and organisation, its state tag and what matched (same customer name, same email domain, same contact email); with nothing to read the box drops to a hairline in `line` and a muted head. The partner never sees it: their side says only that K7 checks for conflicts.

**P16 The protection line (`protect`, `Protection`)** under the status strip of an approved deal: one 6px bar on the track's height, ink for the share of the window that has run, signal once fewer than 14 days remain, muted when it has ended; beneath it the window's dates in mono (`Protected from … to …`, *extended once* when it was) and the days left on the right, in accent when they are few. The same line on both sides; the ledgers carry the end date and the days left as text on the same rule (`is-ending`).

**P17 The dashboard (`synced`, `dash-period`, `dash-cards`)** the partner's deal dashboard (01-requirements §8) opens with the synced line: the pixel (signal when a read-back exists, `line` when none) and one mono sentence, *Last updated from K7's CRM 12 minutes ago · read back every 60 minutes*; nothing on the page is live, every CRM fact is the snapshot the sync wrote. Under it the period as a `seg-choice` (this month · this quarter · this financial year, April to March · last 12 months · all time) on the registration date, and the seven cards of FR-DASH-01 on section 09's `kpis` counters: deals, open, won (signal when any), lost, pipeline value, won value, win rate. Money sets a size down and a width up (`n.is-money`, ₹ in the Indian short forms: ₹12.5 L, ₹2.4 Cr; the full figure in the title). Won and lost are links into the ledger's views; the status views under the charts are `seg-choice` links (`a.is-on`).

**P18 The outcome tag (`OutcomeTag`)** what the CRM says about a deal, in six words: Open and Awaiting approval on `tag-state`, Won solid, Lost and Closed closed, On hold and Not yet in CRM dashed (a state that is nobody's to act on). The CRM's state wins over the portal's once a snapshot exists (FR-DASH-11); before it the portal's own state speaks. On the dashboard ledger, on both deal sheets' CRM facts (`crm-facts`: stage and status reason, estimated value, order value once won, closure dates, when it was read back, the read-back error in `acc`), and on the K7 reconciliation log.

**P19 The money charts (`dash-charts`, `MoneyBars`, `MoneyCols`, `Chart`)** the three charts of FR-DASH-08 on section 09's S9 grid, three up from 300px: pipeline by stage (horizontal bars, counts, the sales process's order with the portal's two pre-CRM states first and on hold last), value by month (columns, every month of the period, empty ones at zero, ₹ short on top), value by product (bars, a deal's value split evenly over its lines, twelve and Other). Ink only, no library; the head carries the title and a mono note, the foot the totals. The dashboard ledger (`dash-table`) is the FR-DASH-02 row: deal and OPP number, customer, product, registered, expected value (the CRM's estimate starred when it differs from the registration), CRM stage, outcome, order value, expected closure, last updated. The K7 side's reconciliation log (`sync-runs`) is a ledger of runs (when, by whom, scanned, returned, changed, missing, errors, the lines in a `details`) with the not-found deals above it in the pending box (P12) and *Sync now* as the one primary action.

**P20 The library (`library`, `lib-group`, `lib-band`, `Library`)** the collateral library (01-requirements §6) is one filed ledger (S7) per product, the programme's own material first, the product's name on the tab with its family in `muted`; inside it the categories are bands (`lib-band`): a mono upper-case row head with the pixel and the count, in the catalogue's order (brochures, datasheets, presentations, case studies, videos, logos and brand, social, email, other). Each item is a row: the title as the link with its description and `#tags` in `acc` mono beneath, the version (`v2`), the file tag (P21), the date of the current file, and *Download* as a small outlined button on the row; the row's sheet repeats the facts on the status strip (P3) with the download as the one primary action and *Open in the browser* beside it for PDFs, images and video. The search and the three selects (product, category, file type) sit in the filters grid (P14) above. Nothing on the page is live: what the partner sees passed the visibility rule on the server, and the download route runs it again. The K7 side's ledger (`lib-k7`) lists every item in every state with its rule in words (`visibilityText`: *Every partner*, or the types, *certified only*, the countries, the regions), the current version linked for a check, the download tally (all time, the last 30 days, the organisations in the title), and *Add an item* leading into the Payload admin, where the editing happens.

**P21 The file tag (`ftag`, `FileTag`)** the deal thread's attachment chip made a part: the extension in upper-case mono (`PDF`, `PPTX`, `MP4`) with the size in `muted` after it, the kind (PDF, presentation, document, spreadsheet, image, video, zip) in the title. On the library rows, the item's strip and the K7 ledger. The item's state for K7 (`CollateralStateTag`) is Published solid, Draft dashed, Retired closed, never signal: nobody must act on a library item, only on one *without a file*, which the desk strip counts.

**P22 Scheme phase (`PhaseTag`, `RegistrationTag`) and the scheme ledger (`sch-table`)** a scheme (01-requirements §9) is a window of dates with slabs; its phase is one `Tag`: *Open for registration* on signal (the partner must act to be in), *Running* solid state, *Starts soon* dashed, *Closed* closed. The organisation's standing beside it: *Registered* solid, *Awaiting K7* quiet for the partner and on signal for the K7 queue, *Declined* closed, *Not registered* dashed. The partner's listing is three filed ledgers (open · running · closed), each scheme a row with its dates, the register-by or end date, the target (the first slab, ₹ in full), the standing and one outlined action (*Register*, *Open*, *Result*). The slabs are a small `dtable` (`slabs`): index, the threshold, the benefit in words, the amount; the slab reached carries the pixel and medium weight, the ones below it drop to `muted`. The terms are plain text with their paragraphs kept (`terms-text`) and the acceptance is the join page's terms box.

**P23 The progress line (`sprog`, `ProgressLine`)** FR-SCH-05 on the protection line's bar: 6px on the track's height, ink for the value won as a share of the top slab, one 2px tick per slab (ink once passed, `muted` ahead), the fill turning signal once the first slab, the target, is reached; beneath it the value won in mono (*₹7,00,000 won so far · provisional* while the scheme runs) and on the right the gap in words (*₹3,00,000 to the target of ₹5,00,000* in accent, or *Slab 1 reached · ₹8,00,000 to slab 2*). The same line shows the published result on both sides. Under it the synced line (P17) says when the CRM was last read back: nothing here is live.

**P24 The result (`result-facts`, `ResultTag`, `PayoutTag`) and the deals counted (`counted`)** FR-ELG-03 as filed facts in the side column: the status tag (*Eligible* solid, *Not eligible* closed, *Under review* dashed, which is also what the partner reads before publication and while a dispute is open), won in the window, the adjustment with its reason, the counted value at medium weight, the target, the slab reached and its benefit, the gap (in accent when not eligible). The deals counted are a filed ledger that sums (deal, customer, products, closed, order value, the total under a double rule), the way the home page's Account does. The dispute (`dispute`) is the pending box (P12) with the pixel in signal while open: the partner's text as a quotation, K7's answer beneath once resolved; the form inside it while the window is open. The payout state is a tag (*Pending* dashed, *Processed* state, *Paid* solid). The K7 ledger (`sch-k7`) lists every scheme with its phase, who it is for, the registrations with the waiting ones in accent and where the results stand (*To compute*, *Computed · to review and publish*, *Published*), the scheme's sheet carries the registrations to decide in the pending box, the results ledger, and the run and the publication on the decision panel (P10); each result has its own sheet for the adjustment, the dispute and the payout.

**The deal thread (`dthread`)** on section 09's message rows (`msg`): one row per entry, who and when, the title of the move (Registered, Approve with changes, Extension granted, Amended by K7, Created in the CRM) beside the name with the state tag it moved the deal to, the text, and the files as tags with their size that open through the deal's own route. Your own side's rows are outlined (`is-you`), the portal's rows dotted and muted (`is-system`). The note form (text and files) sits under the thread while the deal is live; the decision panel (P10) in the side column carries the four decisions with the three change fields, the extension decision, and after approval the amendment form.

**Doors** the sign-in page is section 09's `signin-split` with the partner door (email → code) and the K7 door (Microsoft) side by side; the one the visitor came for leads.
