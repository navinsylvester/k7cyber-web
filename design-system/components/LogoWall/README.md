# LogoWall
Associations, partners and customers as a hairline-gridded wall of 2:1 cells, each holding one logo at up to 40px tall, or the name in mono until the logo file arrives.

**Use** for "Associations & Partnerships" on About K7, the reseller and MSP lists on Partners, and customer logos on Testimonials.

**Provide** {name, logo file, href}. Logos are supplied single-colour or greyscale on the ground; a full-colour logo is placed as supplied, never recoloured.

**Don't** animate or marquee the wall, or fill cells with placeholder marks. Eight cells per row at desktop at most.
