# Account
A ledger that sums. The entries set large on hairlines with their index in `acc-ink`; the last row is the total, under a double rule, its value stamped as a block of ink (`term`). Beside it the statement at lede size, its terms as blocks too, so the paragraph's claim and the ledger's total are the same mark. This is the home page's "Security that answers to you": three commitments, and together a single accountable partner.

**Use** where a few facts add up to one claim and the copy argues it: commitments, a guarantee, a promise and its parts. Three to five entries and one total; the total is the last fact.

**Provide** an ordered list of {k, v} where the last is the total, two to four paragraphs (one term in **double asterisks**), and up to two actions. The index is counted (`01`, `02`), the total's key is a word (`Together`, `In sum`).

**With a panel** (the home page): the statement is filed beside a drawn cover, the same filed FeaturePanel and ReportCover the record sheet uses for its report, on paper so it stands off the panel sheet; its tab names the brand and the kind (`K7 CyberSecurity · commitment`), the cover's tab carries the shield, and the cover reads Accountability · Since 1991 · Three commitments · One partner. The ledger then takes five twelfths, starts level with the panel's top edge, and sizes its words down to fit. On a phone the cover sits over the copy inside the panel, under the ledger.

**Don't** animate the total, add icons, or set more than one block in a paragraph. The double rule is the only rule in the language thicker than a hairline besides the sheet's own `ink` rule; it is for a sum and nothing else. Not for facts that do not add up (Since · 1991 stays a plain fact ledger).

**Build** the `intro` block with `facts` and `account` on (`account-layout`, `account`, `account-sum`, `account-copy`), and `panel` for the filed cover (`has-panel`, `feature two on-paper filed`, `Cover.tsx`). The ledger leads; from 900px the copy sits flush right in the lede's 400px column, under the lede in the sheet head, and below the ledger on a phone; under 480px the index stacks over the value.
