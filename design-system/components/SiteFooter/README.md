# SiteFooter
The last sheet of every page, on `contrast`: the white logo and one sentence, four link columns from the Footer table, the social row, and the legal line.

**Use** once, after the closing CTA sheet, so the ground sequence ends signal → contrast. Columns are Company (Home, About Us, Careers, Contact Us), Offer (Platform, Solutions, Services, Partners), Resources (Insight, Research, Media Room, Support) and Legal (Privacy Policy, EULA). Social links are 36px framed squares with two-letter mono labels that invert on hover.

**Provide** the columns from the Payload `footer` global, the social URLs, and the copyright year.

**Don't** add a newsletter form, badges or an address block here; offices live on Contact Us. Keep the logo at 30px.
