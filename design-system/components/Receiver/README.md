# Receiver
The testimonials as stations on a band, not a carousel. A status line says what is being received (`■ RECEIVING · CH 01/03 · company · tag`, the pixel live); the transmission is the quote at full width, opened with the `acc-ink` mark, with the name and company under it and no portrait; under it the scale, a ruler of ticks with the stations printed on it at their positions (`01 · company`), the current one inverted with its pixel, as a radio prints its station names on the dial. The needle sweeps the scale in steps toward the next station over the dwell (24 jumps, never eased), and when it arrives the quote is replaced with no transition and the pixel on the new station blinks twice: the word arrives, it is stamped. Every transmission is laid out in the same cell (`rx-txs`), so the band keeps the tallest quote's height and an arrival moves nothing below it; trim each quote to its strongest four lines so that height is small. Hover or focus holds the band; pressing a station tunes to it. Under prefers-reduced-motion nothing scans, the status reads Received and the needle rests on the station.

**Two bands**: praise and complaints, on one receiver, flipped by the band switch on the status line (`rx-band`, the same segmented control as the header's theme toggle, each position with its count). Nobody shows the second band; we do. On it the status line reads *Interference* in `acc-ink` with the note *Nobody shows these. We do, and we answer every one.*, every station keeps its quote and byline, and carries K7's reply under them: the label `K7 · reply` and one line as a block of ink (`rx-reply`, `term`). The reply is genuine and fair first and funny second: it concedes what is true, says what we did or will do, and never argues with the customer. An empty band says so in the transmission (*No interference on this band. Yet.*). The switch retunes to the band's first station; `sentiment` on each item picks its band. Today the items are entered by hand; the plan is to feed both bands from reviews automatically.

**Use** on the home page for the customers sheet. Elsewhere a single quote is the Quote part.

**Provide** two or more items of {quote, name, company, tag, sentiment, reply}, verbatim and trimmed to the strongest four lines, and the dwell in seconds (default 10). The company is the station's name; the name is the byline. Until the approved quotes arrive, in-house drafts may stand with a role for the name, a kind of organisation for the company and `· draft` on the tag; they are never published as a customer's words.

**Don't** fade or slide the quote, add portraits or logos, ease the needle, or run the scan under reduced motion. Signal is the pixel on the needle, the live pixel and the stamp, nothing else; the current station inverts, as hover does.

**Build** the `Testimonials` block with `receiver` on (`Receiver.client.tsx`; `rx`, `rx-status`, `rx-txs`, `rx-tx`, `rx-scale`, `rx-needle`, `rx-stations` in `bundle.css`). The stations sit on the scale from 820px and flow as a wrapped row under the ticks below it.
