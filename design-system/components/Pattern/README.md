# Pattern
Three textures, never decoration: PT1 Registration (32px tile, 8px plus, `pattern-mid` ink, masked at 100°) on the hero sheet; PT2 Seven hatch (20px tile, one stroke at the 7's angle, `pattern-min` ink, masked at 260°, strongest top right) on the contrast sheet; PT3 Dot field (24px tile, 2px square, `pattern-max` ink, no mask) only inside figure frames.

**Use** one pattern per sheet at most, in currentColor so it follows the ground, fading away from text through a linear mask.

**Provide** nothing beyond the sheet; the pattern is part of Hero, the contrast "record" sheet, and Placeholder.

**Don't** use patterns on research or product pages, or over imagery.
