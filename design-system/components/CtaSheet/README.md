# CtaSheet
The one `signal` sheet a page is allowed: the closing call to action with a headline, one paragraph and the actions, before the footer.

**Use** at the end of every content page, where the doc places its final [Enquire], [Request Demo] or [Request Quote]. The primary button inverts to `on-signal` on this ground; the line button and text link read in `on-signal`.

**Provide** the headline (default "Security that answers to you."), the paragraph, and up to three actions whose first is the page's form.

**Don't** use `signal` as a ground anywhere else on the page, and don't add imagery or patterns to this sheet.
