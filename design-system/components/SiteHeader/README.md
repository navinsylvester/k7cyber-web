# SiteHeader
The sticky 60px bar with the logo at 36px (until 2026-10-21 it was a 26px box of which half was the file's clear space, 13px of mark, and read as small beside the nav; the files are now trimmed to the mark and it is the heaviest thing on the bar, level with the 36px frames beside it), the eight top-level items from the Header Menu table, the theme toggle (one 36px frame with the theme glyph; it was a Dark | Light group until 2026-10-16) and the one primary action, plus a mega-menu panel that opens on a `.g-panel` sheet beneath it.

**Use** on every page. On the homepage (since 2026-11-21, HomeLanding) the bar takes the landing's layout and keeps everything else: 72px on the landing's 1328px column and gutter, the logo on the column's left edge and the menu flush with its right edge, where the hero's lines end (its gaps closing from 30px to 22px as the page narrows), no theme toggle and no primary action, dark in either theme (`is-home`, `data-theme="dark"` on the header; the rules in `src/app/(frontend)/home.css`). Items with children (Platform, Solutions, Services, Resources, About Us, Why K7) are buttons that open the mega menu; Partners and Support are links. The open item is underlined 2px in `ink`, exactly like the current page. The mega menu has one column per Sub Menu 1 entry, each headed by a `label` with its count, listing Sub Menu 2 pages as rows that invert and indent on hover. A column with no children (Managed Security Services, K7 ISAP, Certifications) is a single row under its parent's label.

**Provide** the nav tree (from the Payload `header` global), the current path, the primary CTA (Request demo), and the two logo files: the white variant in dark, the full-colour variant in light. The theme toggle writes `data-theme` on `<html>` and stores it under `k7-theme`.

**Open over the page** (since 2026-11-21): the mega panel and the mobile list (the site's and the support portal's) lie over the page under the bar, never pushing it down: absolute in the sticky header, their hairline on the header's, at most the rest of the viewport tall and scrolling inside, closed off by a 1px `ink` rule, so they open under the bar wherever the page is scrolled.

**Don't** show more than one panel, add icons to nav items, or shrink the logo under 36px (28px under 520px, where the bar keeps only the action and the Menu button). Below 1040px the nav collapses behind a Menu button and opens the mobile list (24px rows with a pixel on the current page).

**Build** `src/Header/Component.client.tsx` from the `header` global: the bar, the mega panel on `g-panel`, the mobile list behind the Menu button, and the theme toggle as one button (`theme-btn`, Icon `theme`) that flips `data-theme`; no menu library.
