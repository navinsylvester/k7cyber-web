# SupportParts
The support-portal parts from section 09 (reference CSS: `src/app/(portal)/portal.css`, the way section 08 lives in `research.css`). Two audiences share one language: customers get fewer, larger things on the sheet rhythm of the site; agents get the ledger. The portal header is SiteHeader's shape with the account on the right (the bell, the avatar, *Sign out*); under 1040px the links move into the menu, under 760px the name goes, and under 520px the bar's Dark | Light group goes too (the menu carries the theme), so a phone keeps the mark, the bell, the avatar, *Sign out* and *Menu* on one line.

**S1 Case number (`tid`)** the identifier reads like the Labs identifier: `CS-` dim, the unique part first and full weight, mono. `tid-display` is the large form on a case head.

**S2 State tag** a `Tag` for the customer's state, one of four words: Received, Being worked on, Waiting for you, Closed. Solid for the current state; `tag-signal` only for *Waiting for you*, because that is the one state that asks the customer to act; closed at 60% ink. Agents see the Dynamics status reason instead, with the Critical priority as the only signal mark on a row (`crit`).

**S3 Case track (`track`)** four cells in a row (Received · In progress · Your turn · Closed), 6px bars: done in `ink`, current in `signal`, ahead in `line`. One sentence under it says what happens next. Customer pages only; the agent sees the status reason.

**S4 Case row (`case-row`)** the customer's list: number and date in mono on the left, the title and a one-line preview in the middle, the state tag and arrow on the right. An IndexRow in a ledger shape: hover is the inversion, the title link covers the row.

**S5 Conversation (`msg`)** the customer's thread: avatar, name, relative time, the body at 16px with a 66ch measure, attachments as tags. The customer's own messages carry an outlined avatar, K7's a solid one. Newest first. Emails on the case are always shown; notes only when they were written through the portal (subject `Portal:` or `Reply to customer`), so internal notes never leave the desk.

**S6 Timeline (`tl`)** the agent's thread: timestamp column in mono, a kind tag (Email, Internal note, Portal note, Call, Task), the author, the subject, and the body behind a summary (+/−). Internal notes carry a dashed tag.

**S7 Ledger (`dtable` in the workspace)** the saved view's columns as mono heads, sortable through the head links, the case number as the row link, dates DD-MM-YYYY, status as a tag. Pages of 25; the pager and the count sit in `ledger-foot`. Where a list has a results line (products, customers) it is filed: the line is the ledger's tab and the heads sit on a band (DataTable); the case views keep their count in the foot.

**S8 Action panels (`acts`)** the right column of a case: each action is a `details` block with a short form (note, reply, status, assign, resolve, cancel, reopen, queue, call, task). Confirmation is a checkbox inside the form, never a browser dialog.

**S9 Charts (`bars`, `cols`, `trend`)** dashboards on the ledger grid: horizontal bars for categories (sorted by size, twelve plus "Other"), columns for short series, an inline SVG line with a signal end-point for trends. Ink only; Critical is signal.

**S10 Workspace bar (`wsbar`)** the sitemap of the mobile app as research-style tabs under the header, with the quick search on the right; the tabs are 40px and cut on the 7 like every tab. The current section joins the sheet's ground.

**S11 Choice tiles (`choice`)** radio groups as tiles for the choices a customer makes with one tap: what the case is about, individual or organisation. The chosen tile inverts; hover on the chosen tile goes signal.

**S12 KPI card (`kpic`)** the manager's counter on a hairline grid: the measure in `label`, the number at width 62, a 4px meter of progress to the target, the target line and the change against the previous period in mono. "Below target" and the number go `acc-ink`, and the meter `signal`, only when the target is missed; hover is the inversion. Cards link to the page that explains them.

**S13 SLA meter (`slam`)** a 6px bar of the share of target used with the time left in mono: ink while on track, hatched when at risk, `signal` when past target, dim when paused, and the state word in `fmtLeft` ("1 h 20 m left", "overdue 3 h", "met in 25 min"). Compact form inside ledger cells.

**S14 SLA strip (`slastrip`)** five counters in a row: on track, at risk, past target, paused, unanswered; the two that mean a breach go `acc-ink` when non-zero. Each links into the board filtered to that state.

**S15 Engineer card (`eng`)** one engineer on the hairline grid: avatar, name, the load line, six measures in a 3 × 2 grid (resolve SLA, respond SLA, FCR, median resolve, median respond, per day), and a foot of counts where "past target" alone reads `acc-ink`. The name link covers the card; hover inverts. The table layout (`dtable`) is the same data with sortable heads.

**S16 Board row (`board-row`)** the prevention board: the case title with the signal pixel when past target, its facts in `muted`, the rules' verdicts as dashed tags, the two meters, and inline forms for reassign, remind and queue. The two-series trend (`trend2`) draws the second series dashed with a legend, never a second hue.

**S17 Score tag (`score`)** a customer's 1–5 rating as a Tag: "2/5 · Unhappy". A low score is the one state that goes `tag-signal`; a satisfied score is `tag-solid`; the middle is outlined; no rating is dashed. The words come from the five-point scale (Very unhappy … Very happy).

**S18 Care tag (`care`) and mark (`care-mark`)** the band a customer sits in, read before the first line of a reply: *Handle with care* on `signal`, *Watch* in solid ink, *Happy customer* outlined at 78%. The tag sits beside the customer's name on a case head and a contact or account page; the 8px mark stands before the name in ledgers and lists. The title carries the reason. No second hue: the three bands read by word and weight.

**S19 Care band (`care-band`)** one line under a case head when the band is high or watch: the tag, the reason in a sentence ("Rated their last case CS-… 2/5 three days ago."), the count and a History link. A 2px `signal` top rule for high.

**S20 Rating scale (`rate`)** five choice tiles in a row, the number large in mono with its word beneath; the chosen tile inverts and goes `signal` on hover, like S11. Large on the customer's closed case and the emailed survey, compact (numbers only) in the agent's action panel.

**S21 Distribution (`dist`)** the five scores as horizontal bars, 5 on top, count and share in mono; the low rows are `signal`, the satisfied rows ink, the neutral row hatched. The follow-up queue (`fu-row`) is a board row with the customer first, the score and the comment, and inline forms to take and close the follow-up.

**S22 Reason picker (`ReasonSelect`)** one native select, the sixteen reasons grouped under their four areas (the product, product support, licensing & sales, other). The customer sees it in their own words on the survey ("It slows my computer down"), optional; the desk sees the short labels ("Slows the computer"), and the manager closing a follow-up must pick one. Underlined like every portal select, never a widget.

**S23 Cause tag (`cause`)** what a low score was about as an outlined Tag with an 8px square before the words: "Support · Took too long to resolve". The square carries the area by weight: ink for the product, `signal` for support (the desk's own failing is the one that goes signal), hatched for licensing, outlined for other. A small "customer" suffix when it is the customer's own pick, not the desk's call; dashed "Not classified" until somebody says.

**S24 Area stack (`stack`, `stack-rows`)** one 14px bar split by area on a shared scale, the total in mono at the right: a row per product family, per week or per repeat customer. Segments use the same weights as the cause square, plus a dotted muted segment for the unclassified; each segment can link into the filtered ledger. A `legend.areas` key sits under the first stack on a page.

**S25 Item tag (`item-tag`)** the product desk's item reads like a case: `PD-` dim, the digits full weight, mono (`Pid`). Kind and bridge state share one Tag ("Defect · Tracked") with the tracker key and status beside it in mono (`tracker-ref`). `tag-signal` only where someone must act now: a question waiting on the desk (seen by support), a raised item past its triage target (seen by principal support). Released is solid; declined and closed at 60% ink. The priority is a quiet tag; P1 alone goes signal.

**S26 Product band (`product-band`)** one line under a case head while an item is active: the number, the item tag, team · owner · priority · version in `muted`, whose turn it is, and the one action that fits (Answer, Tell the customer, Open item). A 2px `signal` top rule when the item needs information from the desk.

**S27 Hand-off thread (`thread`)** the item's stream in the timeline shape, newest first: timestamp column in mono, the side as an avatar (support outlined, principal support outlined with a dot, engineering and the Lab solid), dashed tags for state and tracker entries, a signal tag for a question, dotted muted entries for what the portal did (mirror results). Text at 15px, evidence as tags. Nothing in it reaches a customer.

**S28 Impact strip (`impact`)** five counters under the item head on hairlines: linked cases, B2B accounts, verticals, first raised, last raised. The case count goes `acc-ink` when demand grew after triage.

**S29 Clocks (`slam`, reused)** the product side's meters in the SLA meter's shape: the triage clock on a raised item (working hours against the priority's target) and the update cadence while tracked (working days since the last activity). Hatched at risk, `signal` past target, met once triaged, dim while paused.

**Use** on `/support` (customers), `/support/workspace` (agents) and `/support/product` (principal support). Customer pages open with a `help-hero` or a compact `ws` sheet; workspace pages are always `ws` sheets so the data sits near the fold.

**Provide** the case row (number, title, status reason, priority, dates), the timeline entries with their audience flag, the view's columns, and the chart series.

**Don't** show Dynamics status reasons, owners or SLA flags to customers; grade engineers by colour (the composite orders cards, it is never shown as a traffic light); put more than one primary button in a view; colour states by hue (they read by word and weight; the care band is signal, solid or outlined, never red, amber and green; the four areas of a low score are ink, signal, hatched and outlined, never four colours); let a customer see a note that was not written through the portal; show a customer their own care band; show a customer anything from an item's thread; colour the two desks (they read by avatar weight: outlined support, solid product).

**S20 Agent forms** (tester round 1, 2026-10-24) a field's label in the portal is 12px in full ink, not the site forms' 11px muted label, in the sheets, the filters and the case action panels alike (the panels' old 10px is gone); a form's numbered section titles (`block-title`) take weight 500; legends read the same as labels. The new case, new contact and new account forms open with the `req-note` line; required labels carry the accent asterisk (Field). A refused submit comes back with every value still chosen, the flash naming each problem, and the failing fields `is-invalid` with the rule as their error text; the browser's own refusal reads the same through `:user-invalid`. Phone inputs carry the rule as a `pattern` and its words as the `title`.

**S21 Case head facts** under the customer: *Account* (a contact customer's organisation, linked), *Phone* (a `tel:` link: the contact's mobile, else phone; for an account, its primary contact's, else the account's), *Email*, then *Serial key*, or the account's *Licence key* when the case has none. The field is *Serial key* on the head, the Details tab, *Edit details* and the new case form; *Incident ID* sits on Details and in both forms; *OS version* (2026-11-18) sits beside the product version on Details, in *Edit details* and on both new case forms (the customer's reads *Operating system*). A merged case's head adds *Merged into* with the surviving case's number, linked. Rows with no value are left out.

**S30 Section heading (`sub-head`)** (2026-10-28, both portals) a section's heading stands on a line, so sections read apart without boxes: its own `line` hairline under the words with a 32 × 2px `signal` segment at its start (the record rail's 2px rule kept short; `on-signal` on a signal sheet). Over a block that opens on its own ink rule (a ledger in `table-wrap`, `mini-rows`, `meta-rows`, `case-list`, `convo`, `tl`, `board`, `events`, `acts`, `empty`, `kpis`, `slastrip`, `rule-lines`, `item-rows` and the rest listed in `portal.css`) the heading drops its hairline and the segment lies on that rule, so there is never a double line. The KPI settings' section titles (`settings-section h2`) take the same rule. A new part that opens on an ink rule joins the list.

**S31 Email composer (`rt-bar`, `rt-area`, `file-list`)** (2026-11-18, testers' notes of 29-10) the case sheet's *Email the customer* panel. Over the message, a formatting bar of hairline tiles in mono caps: **B** *I* <u>U</u>, the two lists, Link and Clear, then Font and Size as underline-less tile selects, then six colour swatches. A tile inverts on hover and stays inverted while its mark is on (`aria-pressed`), like `seg-choice`; a pressed tile goes `signal` on hover. Link opens a line under the bar (the address, *Add link* / *Change link*, *Remove link*, *Cancel*), never a browser dialog; Enter applies it. The message sits on light paper in either theme (`data-theme="light"` on the area) in the email's own Arial 14px inside a `line` hairline frame (in the light theme the paper is the page's colour), because it shows the email as the customer reads it; the link blue and the sizes are the ones sent. The swatches are the one place hues appear in the portal: they are content the agent writes, not the interface. Pasting brings words without another page's styles. Under the message, the attachments picker takes several files and lists them with their sizes (`file-list`: name, size in mono, hairlines) once there are two; a file Dynamics would refuse (a blocked extension, more than ten, more than 15 MB in all) is refused in the browser with the reason as the field's error. Without JavaScript the message is a plain textarea. *Send email* reads *Sending…* and is disabled while the post runs (`SubmitButton`, as on the partner desk). In the timeline and the customer's conversation (`tl-body`, `msg-body`), a link in a body is underlined at 1px with a 3px offset and takes the accent on hover, and lists keep their bullets and numbers: the reset had made both read as plain text.

**S33 State and City (`GeoPicker`)** (2026-11-26, testers' note: State and City not filtered by letter) the contact and account forms' state → city cascade as two *Type to filter* fields (Field): the state's list, then its cities fetched when it is chosen (Tamil Nadu 291 entries on UAT, Andhra Pradesh 453); a new state empties the city and the city waits, switched off, until its list arrives; the region follows the state, read-only. A city name UAT lists twice (its own set next to the production set the seed copied in) is offered once, the newest record, or the one the contact or account already has; the server accepts either record.

**S34 Locked by Dynamics (field security)** (2026-11-26, testers' note: Bug ID not getting added in Incident ID) Dynamics secures some columns for chosen field security profiles: on cases Incident ID, Module and Sub module (Level 2 Users) and Product version (Level 1 and Level 2), on RFEs and defects the YODIZ id (System Administrators only). The portal reads the signed-in agent's rights on them (`RetrievePrincipalAttributePrivileges`, kept five minutes) and shows a column they may not set as a switched-off field (Field) with the hint *Locked: Dynamics field security does not let your user set this.*; one muted line under the case facts names the columns Dynamics hides from them. If Dynamics still refuses a secured column (rights unknown), the write goes again without the secured columns and the flash names what was not saved; the product desk's log says *left out: field security*.

**S32 Merge panel** (2026-11-18) *Merge into another case* sits above *Cancel case* on an active case: one muted line says what happens and that it cannot be undone; the customer's other active cases are `choice` tiles in one column (number in `tid`, the title, then status, opened and owner as the tile's `d` line), with *Another case* as the last tile for a typed number; nothing is chosen until the agent picks, and a confirmation tick names the case being merged; *Merge case* reads *Merging…* while Dynamics moves the timeline (10 to 20 seconds). The agent lands on the surviving case with a flash naming the merged one. A merged case keeps its sheet: the head's *Merged into* row, and a *Merged* panel in place of *Reopen*. A customer following an old link to it lands on the surviving case with one line saying the two were joined.
