# ContactLocation
An office: the city as a block heading, the postal address, a meta ledger of numbers and addresses, and a framed map.

**Use** on Contact Us for India, Singapore and UAE, in that order. Phone numbers are `tel:` links in mono; email addresses are selectable text in sans (no `mailto:`, which fails for many visitors).

**Provide** {city, legal entity, address, telephone, fax, sales, support, toll-free, map}. Maps are static images in a `ph` frame with a caption, linking to the map provider.

**Don't** embed interactive maps inline, or merge offices into one block.
