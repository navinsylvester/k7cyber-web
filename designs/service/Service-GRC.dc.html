02/05Overview

Strategy, not paperwork.

The K7 Governance, Risk and Compliance Consulting team leverages K7's multi-decade expertise as a cybersecurity provider to analyse an organisation's digital infrastructure, policies and processes, to achieve enterprise-wide strategic objectives and avoid operational inefficiency, cost escalation, cybersecurity gaps and non-compliance penalties.

03/05Benefits

Benefits.

Two kinds of risk, managed.

Financial Risk Management

Identify security gaps in financial processes that could result in financial malfeasance and leakage of funds.

Operational Risk Management

Policies, standard operating procedures, operation manuals and cybersecurity training to ensure security policies and standards are maintained.

04/05Focus areas

Focus areas.

Infrastructure, process and compliance, in that order.

01 · Infrastructure

K7 GRC evaluates enterprise digital infrastructure by:

  1. Conducting information security audits
  2. Performing Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing
  3. Verifying digital communication integrity
  4. Assessing cybersecurity risk from Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) and Shadow IT
02 · Process

K7 GRC assesses digital business processes for:

  1. Adherence to industry best practices
  2. Adherence to internal standards and policies
  3. Use of appropriate technologies and protocols
  4. Hardware and software lifecycle management
  5. Business continuity management
