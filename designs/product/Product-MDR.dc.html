02/06Overview

A command centre. Until now, impossible.

Enterprises are struggling to manage cybersecurity and compliance across a digital ecosystem of multiple platforms, solutions, services and devices, further complicated by the deployment of multiple cybersecurity solutions.

Creating a cybersecurity command centre with comprehensive event visibility and proactive threat mitigation across the organisation has been impossible to achieve, until now.

K7 Seventh Sense MDR enables enterprises to keep pace with the constant evolution of the cyberthreat landscape through continuous threat discovery, defence assessment and cybersecurity posture improvement. Unifying your existing Security Operations Centre (SOC) with proprietary and third-party cybersecurity platforms, K7's services deliver what no single product or activity can: turnkey enterprise cybersecurity across internal and external operating environments.
