02/06The platform

One platform. Endpoint to SOC.

Most cybersecurity tools are designed to detect one part of the problem: an endpoint event, an identity anomaly, a data movement or a threat. Real risk often lies in the connections between them.

K7 Seventh Sense goes beyond the obvious to understand security.
