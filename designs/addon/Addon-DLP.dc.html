02/05Overview

Volume, velocity, variety.

The volume, velocity and variety of data generated by data-driven organisations have emphasised the need for secure data storage and access, to protect both Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and confidential business information.

K7 Data Loss Protection ensures critical enterprise data assets are protected from unauthorised access and modification, avoiding penalties for non-compliance with data security regulations and loss of competitive advantage.
