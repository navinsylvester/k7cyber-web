# The partner portal as built (M1–M11, 2026-10-22 to 24): routes, doors, rules, how to run it

What exists in the repo after the skeleton (M1), onboarding (M2), organisation (M3), deal registration (M4), the dashboard (M5), the collateral library (M6), schemes with eligibility (M7) the notification templates, the approval SLA and the reports (M8) and the hardening (M9: the Operations page, the health route, the
per-feature CRM switches, the runbook `05-staging-and-go-live.md`), how it fits the
support portal it stands on, and what to check before trusting a page. `02-build-plan.md` is the plan and the cross-session state; this file
is "what is built", like the support portal's `09-portal-guide.md`. Dynamics is touched by two jobs: the partner account and its contact on
approval, and the end customer, its contact and the opportunity on a deal's approval, both through the outbox (§5), only when the mirror is
switched on. The read-back of the opportunity's stage and value (the dashboard) arrives with M5.

## 1. Run it
```
docker compose up -d                                   # Postgres for Payload (the portal's system of record)
pnpm dev                                               # site + admin + both portals on :3000
pnpm partner:seed-dev you@example.com "Your Name" [--crm] [--collateral] [--editor] [--schemes]  # once: the PP-TEST organisation (K7P-00000), three PP-TEST products and you as its Partner Admin; --crm queues its account job (pending) for the drain route or a Run button; --collateral files four PP-TEST items with one-page PDFs (M6); --editor makes pp-test-editor@k7computing.com, a Payload Marketing editor for the /admin side of the library, with PARTNER_DEV_EDITOR_PASSWORD from .env
```
`.env` needs the support portal's keys (`AUTH_SECRET`, Postgres, and for the K7 door the Entra app or `SUPPORT_DEV_AGENT=probe`)
plus `PARTNER_PORTAL_ENABLED` (on in development unless `0`; production needs `1`, or every `/partners/portal` page is a 404).
Every absolute link the portal hands out (notices, the applicant's status link, the certificate's verify link and QR) comes from
`partnerPublicUrl()` (`src/lib/partner/public-url.ts`), read at run time: `PARTNER_PUBLIC_URL`, else `AUTH_URL`, else `NEXT_PUBLIC_SERVER_URL`
(fixed into the build, so it said `localhost` on the tunnel until 2026-10-27, B-02). Rows and PDFs written before then keep their old links.
`SUPPORT_DEV_SHOW_CODE=1` shows the partner's one-time code on the verify page while no `SMTP_URL` is set, under the same
localhost-or-allowlist rule as the support door. `SUPPORT_MAIL_ALLOWLIST` applies to partner codes and notifications too.

Then open http://localhost:3000/partners/join (apply), http://localhost:3000/partners/portal (partner users) or
http://localhost:3000/partners/portal/sign-in?staff=1 (K7 staff). A K7 person opens the queues only when listed as Channel Manager
in the settings (`/admin/globals/partner-settings` → Staff the first time; then `/partners/portal/k7/settings`), or through the probe
door with `devStaffPass` on. `PARTNER_SMS_PROVIDER` is empty by default: the join step records the mobile and marks its verification
skipped. Tests: `pnpm test:int` runs `partner-session` (scope, roles, settings, the code doors) and `partner-registration` (the
application machine, validation, the CRM mapping), `partner-organisation` and `partner-deals` (the deal machine, the form and the gate,
protection, the conflict matcher, the opportunity mapping), `partner-dashboard` and `partner-collateral` (the visibility rule, the file kinds, the
grouping and filters, the Range header, the size cap) without a tenant; with `PARTNER_DB_TESTS=1` also `partner-notify`,
`partner-notify-model`, `partner-sla` and `partner-reports` (M8: the catalogue against the code's events, the templates, the clock in working hours, the report maths); with `PARTNER_DB_TESTS=1` also `partner-notify`, `partner-m8-db` (a template on a bell row, the digest and the retention, the SLA sweep once per clock),
`partner-m9-db` (M9: a job held pending by its feature switch, the read-back refusing when its switch is off, a run recorded, housekeeping),
`partner-onboarding`, `partner-scope`, `partner-deals-db`, `partner-sync-db` and `partner-collateral-db` (two organisations against eight items in every state and rule; the file served whole and by range; runs under the Node environment because Payload's type sniffing fails under jsdom) (register → request → resubmit → approve with changes → conflict for the other
organisation → extension → sweep, against Postgres, rows removed again). Without a tenant also `partner-m9` (M9: every status machine as a
full matrix, the scope rule's corners, the cron guard, the code limiter, the CRM feature switches, the readiness checks, the conflict
matcher and the eligibility maths over anonymised fixtures) and `partner-routes` (reads every route and server action under the portal
from the source and fails when one lacks its door). The scheduled routes (`05-staging-and-go-live.md` §3) take `POST` with
`Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>` through `src/lib/cron-auth.ts` (constant-time, a secret under 16 characters opens nothing), and
every run is a `partner-job-runs` row; `GET /api/partners/health` is the monitor's question; `/partners/portal/k7/operations` the page.

## 2. Sign-in: one Auth.js instance, a third kind of session (`src/auth.ts`)
| door | who | how | session |
|---|---|---|---|
| `partner` | partner users | the support portal's signed one-time code (`src/lib/support/otp.ts`) with the purpose `partner-sign-in` and its own cookie (`k7-partner-otp`, path `/partners`, `src/lib/partner/otp.ts`); the code goes to the email of an existing `partner-users` row whose organisation is approved and who is not disabled; the first sign-in activates an invited user | `kind: 'partner'`, `partnerUserId`, `orgId`, `roles[]` |
| `microsoft-entra-id` / `dev-agent` | K7 staff | the support portal's doors unchanged; the partner-portal roles are **not** Dataverse roles but the `staff` list of the Payload global `partner-settings` (work email → roles), so a Marketing Admin needs no CRM access | `kind: 'agent'`; roles resolved per request by `staffRolesFor` |

A code's purpose is inside the signed payload, so a partner code never opens the customer door or the reverse (pinned by the
test). One cookie carries one session: signing in on one side ends the other side's session. The two sides ignore each
other's sessions rather than looping: `/support` treats a partner session as signed out (`proxy.ts`, `requireCustomer`,
`optionalSession`, the support header), `/partners/portal` sends a customer session to the partner door and an agent session to
the K7 side. The door sends a partner session home only while it can still be used (`signInLanding` in
`src/lib/partner/session-model.ts`): a session whose organisation is suspended or whose user is disabled or removed stays on the
door with the reason (B-01, it looped with `requirePartner` before), and asking for a new code ends that session first.

`src/proxy.ts` (Next 16's proxy, Node runtime) gates `/partners/portal/k7/*` to an agent session and the rest of
`/partners/portal/*` to a partner session; `sign-in` and `verify` are public. The role check needs Payload and happens in the
page (`requireK7Role`). The `partner` provider loads its Payload lookup lazily so the proxy's bundle does not carry the config.

reports) is not there yet. Products for the deal form are edited in the Payload admin (`/admin/collections/partner-products`,

file under Files, each upload a version) by a Payload user with the marketing editor role; the portal only reads it.

## 7. Rules the code enforces
- A partner page reads nothing without `me.scope` (`orgWhere`); an empty scope throws rather than widening.
- Roles and status are re-read from Payload per request; the JWT's copy is a hint for the proxy only.
- K7 roles come from `partner-settings`, never from Dynamics; development sign-ins hold every role unless `devStaffPass` is off, never in production.
- Mail leaves only through `sendMail`: `SMTP_URL`, `SUPPORT_MAIL_ALLOWLIST`, the partner sender. No SMTP: the console, and the on-screen code on localhost or with the allowlist set.
- An application moves only along 01-requirements §3.2 (`status.ts`); every move writes an audit row and emails the applicant; a rejection or a clarification needs a note.
- An application is submitted only with the contact email verified by code; the mobile is verified by SMS when a gateway exists and otherwise marked skipped where the Channel Manager sees it.
- The status link is number + an unguessable key; a wrong key shows nothing. The join cookie carries the same key, httpOnly, scoped to `/partners/join`.
- Approval refuses an email that is already a partner user somewhere; the organisation and the Partner Admin are created together, the partner ID from the counter.
- A partner user is read through the scope even by id (`loadMember`); the Partner Admin's posts re-check the role and the rules on the server, so an organisation never loses its last standing admin and a person never disables or demotes themselves. The email is the sign-in identity and is not edited: remove and add instead.
- The legal name, GSTIN and PAN change only through a proposal a Channel Manager approves; one proposal at a time; an empty change is refused; a decline needs a note. The details (trade name, address, website, regions) change at once and are audited field by field.
- Suspension needs a reason, which every user of the organisation gets by email; the door refuses their codes and `requirePartner` re-reads the organisation on every request, so open sessions end at once. Reinstatement clears the reason.
- Dataverse is written only by the outbox, as the service identity, only when `partner-settings.crm.enabled` is on, only to UAT (production refused in code); the job is recorded whatever happens and can be re-run.
- A deal is registered only by an active Partner Admin or Sales user of an approved organisation (Technical users read the list); with the certificate switch on, only with a valid certificate or a waiver. A deal is read through the scope even by number (`loadDeal`); the files through the scope on the partner side and the approver role on the K7 side.
- A deal moves only along 01-requirements §7.2 (`status.ts`); every move writes an audit row, a thread entry and a notification to the people concerned (the registering user and the organisation's admins; the Deal Desk Approvers). A rejection and a request for information need a note; approve with changes needs a changed value, closure or customer.
- The conflict check runs at submission and again live on the K7 sheet, over the portal's live deals of every organisation (same normalised customer name, same work-email domain, same contact email); the partner is never shown whose they are. The CRM-side check is the settings switch of 03-dynamics-exploration.md §7, not built.
- The partner edits the registration only after a request for information; after approval only K7 amends the value, the closure date or the customer, and either side adds notes and files while the deal is live. One extension per deal, asked for while approved and inside the window; the sweep expires approved deals past their end and leaves a waiting extension to the approver.
- The `K7D-` number is given at submission so the queue, the bell and the thread can name the deal; approval makes it the protected registration ID of FR-DEAL-06.
- The dashboard reads the snapshot and nothing live: the browser never meets the CRM (FR-DASH-04). The sync reads only the opportunities whose ids the portal stored, never a CRM-wide filter (decision 11; FR-DASH-10 deferred), as the service identity, GET only, at most every `crm.syncMinutes` unless forced. Where the CRM and the registration disagree on stage, state or value, the CRM's figure is what the partner sees and the difference is one audit row on the deal (FR-DASH-11); the registration's own fields are not overwritten. An opportunity the CRM no longer returns keeps its last values and is listed on the K7 log.
- Collateral reaches a partner only when the item is published, has a file and its rule holds for the organisation (type, certification, country, region, all of those that are set); the list, the sheet and the download route each apply it, so an item's id leaks nothing. The directory is outside `public/` and the route checks the resolved path stays inside it. Every download is one audit row on the organisation. Payload's single upload limit is 200 MB for the collateral; `maxUpload` keeps Media, report files and deal files at 30, 30 and 10 MB, and the collateral's own cap is the setting (never above 200).

- (M9) A scheduled route opens only to the exact `Bearer CRON_SECRET`, compared in constant time; an unset or short secret opens nothing. Every run, by the scheduler or by hand, is one `partner-job-runs` row. The health route says ok or the failing checks' keys and nothing else.
- (M9) The CRM mirror's master switch has three feature switches under it (the go-live gate, `05-staging-and-go-live.md` §5): a write job whose feature is off waits as pending, no attempt counted, and the read-back refuses, forced or not, until its switch is on.
- (M9) A code leaves at most five times per address and thirty times per client address in fifteen minutes (the first `x-forwarded-for` hop, else `x-real-ip`, else one key for direct calls), on the join page and the partner door alike; the limits live in memory per process.
- (M9) Every page and file under `/partners` is framed only by the site itself (`X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`), never sniffed, the referrer cut to the origin, no device access; the API routes are `no-store`. Housekeeping removes a join draft nobody touched for thirty days and job-run rows older than ninety.
- (M8) What a notification says is the code's text unless a Channel Manager wrote a template for the event; a template is used only when it exists and is on, a blank subject or body keeps the code's half, and a placeholder the event does not fill is refused at save. The delivery rule is the template's, else the catalogue's: at once, in the daily digest, or the bell only; a digest row is mailed once (`digestAt`) and marked even when no mail could leave, so the queue never loops. A test goes only to the editor's own address, through the allowlist like every mail.
- (M8) The approval clock runs in the programme's working hours from the latest submission of a deal or application (or the extension request), never while the item waits on the partner; the breach notice goes once per clock start to the queue's role, the holders' managers and the escalation addresses, and the queue rows, the sheets and the reports read the same clock. Reports are computed from the portal's rows and the CRM snapshots, never live, and each CSV is the same rows for the same period to the same roles.
- A scheme reaches a partner only when published and applying to the organisation's type and regions; the registration and the result are read through the scope; the result is null until the scheme's results are published, and reads "Under review" while a dispute is open. Only a Partner Admin registers (terms accepted, name and time stored) or disputes (inside the window: the scheme's days, else the setting). Results are computed only for a published scheme after its end date, from the deals' CRM snapshots (won, actual close date inside the dates, a named product when any, at the order value), recomputed in place until published with adjustments kept, never after; an adjustment needs its reason; a decline needs a note; a processed or paid payout needs a reference; only an eligible, published result has a payout.
